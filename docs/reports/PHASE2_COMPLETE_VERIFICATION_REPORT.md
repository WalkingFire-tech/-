## 关键类和接口

**EmotionDetector** - 情绪检测器
- `detect(text)` - 检测文本情绪
- 返回: EmotionalState (primary_emotion, confidence, intensity, urgency, confusion, sentiment)

**StereoMemorySystem** - 立体记忆系统
- `store(content, memory_type, importance, ...)` - 存储记忆
- `recall(query, limit)` - 检索记忆
- `get_statistics()` - 获取统计

**RelationshipModel** - 关系模型
- `record_interaction(interaction_type, ...)` - 记录互动
- `get_trust_level()` - 获取信任级别
- `state` - 关系状态 (trust_level, intimacy_level, ...)

**SelfReviewEngine** - 自我评估引擎
- `review(conversation)` - 评估对话
- `get_stats()` - 获取统计
- 返回: ReviewResult (outcome, overall_score, strengths, weaknesses, insights)

**ActivePerceptionEngine** - 主动感知引擎
- `start()` - 启动感知循环
- `stop()` - 停止感知循环
- `get_status()` - 获取状态
